# Privacy policy

Applies to Civitai Image & Video Tag Search version 1.2.0. Updated November 13, 2026.

## Local information

The extension stores your current search words, selected tags, chosen website and media type, and learned tag names, IDs and ratings in local extension storage. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to the extension developer. Removing the extension removes its local storage.

## Requests to Civitai

When live lookup runs, your typed search words are sent to the selected Civitai website, civitai.com or civitai.red, through a Civitai tab. Your existing site session and Civitai's normal network and account handling may apply. Opening a gallery sends the selected numeric tag IDs as URL parameters to Civitai. These requests are subject to Civitai's own privacy practices.

The extension only uses this information to provide the tag-search and gallery-link features you choose. It does not use or transfer it for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated purposes.

## Developer collection and third parties

The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, data sales, or remotely executed code. The developer does not receive your searches, browsing history, credentials, cookies, or tag selections. The extension does not read other websites.

## Permissions

Local storage remembers your choices and tag metadata. Host access is limited to `https://civitai.com/*` and `https://civitai.red/*`, to locate a suitable Civitai tab and perform read-only image-tag lookup.

**Copy link** writes the displayed gallery URL to your clipboard only when clicked. No separate clipboard permission is requested.

## Content settings

The extension supports mature-rated tags and links to Civitai galleries. Civitai account restrictions and content settings determine what you can view. The extension does not change or bypass them.

## Questions

Use [the repository's issue tracker](https://github.com/Aguzokk/civitai-tag-search/issues). Do not include passwords, cookies, private account data, or sensitive search history in a public issue.
